# Code of Conduct

Nuitka Studio aims to maintain a welcoming, practical, and respectful open-source community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when discussing code, design, documentation, and mistakes.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Welcome beginners and explain technical decisions without ridicule.
- Respect privacy and do not repost private information or logs.
- Credit other people's work and preserve required attribution.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, malicious code, hidden telemetry, credential collection, and attempts to bypass repository safety rules are not allowed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community or project.

Report serious conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact method on GitHub. Do not publish sensitive evidence in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible, though complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and other spaces officially used by the project.
